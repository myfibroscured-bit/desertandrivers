Arada Day Trip from Dahab

One valley, one day, properly seen

Most desert trips from Dahab race between photo stops. This one doesn't. Our Arada day trip is a full-day desert safari through the sandstone country of South Sinai — rock inscriptions, a real canyon hike, fossils, 5,000-year-old tombs and a sand dune finale — led by Joseph and Annet together, with over 30 years of Sinai guiding and nearly 20 years living in Dahab between them.

Rock inscriptions & the Double Canyon

Leaving Dahab at 8:00, the jeep safari heads inland toward Wadi Arada. First stop is the Arada Rock of Inscriptions — ancient carvings of camels, hunters and daily Bedouin life scratched into the sandstone. From there we hike and climb through the Double Canyon, including one steep rope-assisted descent, passing a stretch of fossilised coral reef with shell fossils still visible in the rock.

Desert lunch & the Nawamis tombs

While you're in the canyon, our desert chef prepares a fresh lunch on the spot — no restaurant, no menu. After lunch, a short drive brings us to the Nawamis: circular stone tombs built around 5,000 years ago, older than the pyramids and among the oldest roofed structures still standing anywhere on earth.

It's slow travel in the truest sense. One valley, one day, properly seen.

Dragonhead Mountain & Hedoda Sand Dune

We continue to Dragonhead Mountain (Gebel Makhroum) for the view across the sandstone country and the dune fields below, then finish at Hedoda Sand Dune — climb it, sandboard down it, or just sit at the top and watch the light change before the drive back to Dahab.
