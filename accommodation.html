Accommodation

Two trusted hotels on Dahab's boulevard

We work with two partner hotels right on Dahab's seafront boulevard, both a short walk from where our trips begin — handy whether you're arriving the night before, staying on after your journey, or just want a comfortable base in town. Let us know which suits you and we'll help arrange it as part of your booking. Ask us too if you'd prefer a different style of stay — we know a few other options around Dahab as well.

Lidia Hotel

Seafront · South Boulevard

A recently renovated 30-room hotel on the quieter southern stretch of the Dahab boulevard, an easy walk from town's small shops and restaurants. Rooms are en-suite with air-conditioning, a fridge and a kettle; the upstairs rooms open onto balconies over the pool and the Red Sea. Breakfast is included and served in the beachfront restaurant, which also does lunch and dinner.

  • En-suite rooms with A/C, fridge & kettle
  • Courtyard swimming pool, multiple depths
  • Beachfront restaurant, easy water access
  • Breakfast included

Sababa Hotel

Family-Run · Heart of the Boulevard

A small, family-owned hotel right in the centre of the Lighthouse Boulevard, with a 24-hour front desk, a bar and terrace, and free private parking. Breakfast (continental or vegan) and WiFi are included. The restaurant opens straight onto calm bay water, and the Lighthouse Reef — a popular spot for diving and snorkelling — is just a short swim away.

  • 24-hour front desk & room service
  • Bar, terrace & free private parking
  • Steps from the Lighthouse Reef
  • Breakfast included (continental or vegan)
