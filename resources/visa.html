Egypt visa on arrival: what’s changing in 2027

Egypt’s Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities has announced a higher visa fee and a new digital visa system that will roll out airport by airport. Here’s what it means for your trip.

Last updated: November 2026

  • The tourist visa fee rises from $30 to $33 on 1 January 2027.
  • A new digital visa with a QR code replaces the paper sticker, airport by airport.
  • Where the digital system is live, there’s an extra $6 service fee per person.
  • Sharm El Sheikh and Taba, the airports for Dahab, don’t have a date yet.

The visa fee

When you arriveVisa fee
Until 31 December 2026$30 per person
From 1 January 2027$33 per person
At airports with the digital systemVisa fee + $6 service fee

The $33 fee applies at every airport from January 2027, even where the digital system hasn’t arrived yet.

The new digital visa

Instead of buying a paper sticker at the airport, you’ll get a QR code. You can get it:

  • on a dedicated website
  • through a mobile app
  • at self-service kiosks in the arrivals hall

You can sort it before you fly, yourself or through your travel agent. Each QR code is valid for up to seven days from the date it’s issued, so don’t get it too early.

What this means if you’re coming to Dahab

Flying into Sharm El Sheikh, the closest airport to Dahab? There’s no date for the digital system there yet, so for now you’ll get your visa on arrival as usual, just at the new $33 fee from January.

Flying in through Cairo? The digital system is already running there, so budget for the $6 service fee on top of the visa.
