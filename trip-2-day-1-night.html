Overnight Desert Trip from Dahab

Two days, one night — the silence after the fire burns low

Back to Packages

A day trip shows you the desert. A night in it shows you something else entirely — the silence after the fire burns low, and a sky so dark the Milky Way needs no telescope. This two-day desert camping trip from Dahab follows the same route as our Arada day trip, but unhurried: more time at every stop, longer in the canyon, and a night in the desert in the middle of it all. Led by Joseph and Annet together, with over 30 years of Sinai guiding and nearly 20 years living in Dahab between them.

Day 1 — Inscriptions, canyon and camp, taken slowly

Leaving Dahab at 8:00, the jeep safari begins on the inland road toward Wadi Arada — the same start as our day trip, but with a whole extra day ahead, nothing needs to be rushed. We take our time among the ancient rock inscriptions, then hike and climb through the Double Canyon, passing fossilised coral reef along the way. A long desert lunch, a visit to the Nawamis tombs, and an easy walk through Hamata Canyon as the light softens bring us to camp for dinner around the fire.

Where you'll sleep — choose your camp

Zigzag Canyon — the romantic camp

Sheltered between the winding walls of Zigzag Canyon: a candle-lit dinner in the quiet, firelight on the sandstone, and a night wrapped in stone. Made for two.

Open air near Gebel Makhroum — the stargazer's camp

Sleeping in the open desert near Dragonhead Mountain, far from any light pollution. On a clear, moonless night the Milky Way stretches horizon to horizon. Plan around the new moon for the darkest skies.

A day trip shows you the desert. A night in it shows you something else entirely.

Day 2 — Mountains, dunes and the slow road home

A relaxed morning in camp, then Dragonhead Mountain (Gebel Makhroum) for the view across the sandstone country and the dune fields below. The grand finale is Hedoda Sand Dune — sandboarding, climbing it more than once, or simply watching the light move across the desert — before the drive back to Dahab.
